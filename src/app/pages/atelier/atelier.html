Atelier

Marlow cuts a small ready-to-wear floor: coats that clear a knit, trousers with a real thigh, and shirts you can press at home. The pieces on the floor are the collection. There is no separate drop and no waiting list.

Cloth and care

Cloth is named on each piece, with the care it actually needs. Wool is brushed and aired. Cotton is washed. Silk is cleaned, not guessed at.

Fit notes describe the cut. They are not a lab chart, and they are not a promise that two bodies wear one size the same way.
