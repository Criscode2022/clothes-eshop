Atelier

Marlow cuts a small ready-to-wear floor: coats that clear a knit, trousers with a real thigh, and shirts you can press at home. The pieces on the floor are the collection. There is no separate drop and no waiting list.

Cloth is named on each piece, with the care it actually needs. Wool is brushed and aired. Cotton is washed. Silk is cleaned, not guessed at. Fit notes describe the cut. They are not a lab chart, and they are not a promise that two bodies wear one size the same way.

How an order leaves

Shipping is $8, and free when the pieces total at least $200. You can check out as a guest. An account only keeps the order under your name. Payment is the method you choose: at the shop, by bank transfer, or in cash when the parcel arrives. Marlow does not take a card number.

After you place the order, the workroom moves it from placed, to picking, to ready, and then completed. You can still cancel before it is finished.
